About Us

Enhancing natural beauty since day one

Neat’n’Even Beauty Clinic is a Ghanaian beauty brand specialising in professional makeup artistry and beauty services for brides, special occasions, photoshoots, artistes and media personalities.

With a passion for enhancing natural beauty, we create elegant, refined and personalised looks that complement each client’s features, personality and occasion. Our approach combines creativity, precision and professionalism to ensure every client feels confident and beautifully represented.

Our story
Janet Ayisi

Meet the Founder

Janet Ayisi is a Ghanaian professional makeup artist, cosmetologist and beauty entrepreneur, and the founder of Neat’n’Even Beauty Clinic.

She has built the brand around the belief that makeup should not change who you are, but rather enhance your natural beauty and bring out your confidence. Her expertise spans bridal makeup, special occasions, photoshoots, artiste and media makeup, as well as makeup education.
