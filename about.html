About the brand

Beauty that is refined, confidence that is felt

Neat’n’Even Beauty Clinic is a Ghanaian beauty brand specialising in professional makeup artistry and beauty services for brides, special occasions, photoshoots, artistes and media personalities.

Our story

Artistry built around the person in the chair

With a passion for enhancing natural beauty, we create elegant, refined and personalised looks that complement each client’s features, personality and occasion.

Our approach combines creativity, precision and professionalism to ensure every client feels confident and beautifully represented. No two faces are the same, and no two occasions ask for the same thing — so every look begins with a conversation, not a product.

We have had the privilege of working with notable Ghanaian gospel artistes, including Piesie Esther and Shantel Boamah, as well as clients across different areas of the beauty and entertainment industry.

Based in Accra, Ghana, Neat’n’Even is available for bookings across Ghana and internationally. Whether it’s a wedding, special event, photoshoot or artiste engagement, we are ready to bring our artistry wherever your occasion takes you.

Meet the CEO

Janet Ayisi

Founder & CEO

Janet Ayisi is a Ghanaian professional makeup artist, cosmetologist and beauty entrepreneur, and the founder of Neat’n’Even Beauty Clinic.

With a passion for beauty and artistry, Janet has built Neat’n’Even around the belief that makeup should not change who you are, but rather enhance your natural beauty and bring out your confidence.

Her expertise spans bridal makeup, special occasions, photoshoots, artiste and media makeup, as well as makeup education. Through her work, she has had the privilege of working with notable Ghanaian gospel artistes, including Piesie Esther and Shantel Boamah, among others.

Known for her attention to detail, creativity and commitment to professionalism, Janet continues to grow Neat’n’Even into a beauty brand that delivers quality artistry and exceptional client experiences.

Her vision is simple: to create beauty that is refined, confidence that is felt, and an experience that is remembered.
