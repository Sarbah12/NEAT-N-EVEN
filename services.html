Before You Book

The questions we get most

Do you travel outside Accra?
Yes. We take bookings across Ghana and internationally. Travel, accommodation and early call times are quoted on top of the service rate, and we’ll always put that in writing before you commit.
How far in advance should I book?
For weddings, as early as you can — peak wedding months fill first. For sessions and shoots, a week or two is usually comfortable, but do ask about short-notice availability; we can often accommodate it.
Do you offer a bridal trial?
Yes, and we recommend it. A trial lets us test the look in daylight and on camera, adjust the finish for your skin, and lock in exactly what happens on the morning so there are no surprises.
What if I have sensitive or acne-prone skin?
Tell us at the consultation. Skin preparation is adjusted to your type, and we can work with non-comedogenic and fragrance-free products. If you have a known allergy, we’ll patch test in advance.
Do you do makeup for darker skin tones?
Always. Foundation matching across the full range of deep and rich skin tones is core to how we work — no grey cast, no ashy finish, no mask line at the jaw.
Can you cover the whole bridal party?
Yes. Bridesmaids, mothers and family are booked at a per-face rate, and we bring additional artists for larger parties so the morning stays on schedule.
How do I secure my date?
A deposit confirms the booking. Until that’s received the date stays open to other enquiries, so if you’re certain, it’s worth confirming early.
