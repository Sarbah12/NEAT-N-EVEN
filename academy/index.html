What you’ll learn

Eleven modules, start to finish

The class covers the full professional workflow — the technique that makes the look, and the practice that makes the business.

  1. Skin preparation & product knowledge
  2. Foundation matching
  3. Flawless base application
  4. Brows made easy
  5. Eyeshadow blending techniques
  6. Contour, blush & highlight
  7. Lash application
  8. Lip combinations
  9. Professional finishing techniques
  10. Client etiquette & hygiene
  11. Content creation tips for beauty professionals

Who it’s for

Whether you’re starting out or sharpening up

01

Beginners

No experience needed. You’ll leave understanding skin, product and the order of a full face.

02

Self-taught artists

Fill the gaps — matching, blending and finishing techniques that separate good from professional.

03

Working professionals

Client etiquette, hygiene standards and content creation to grow the business behind the brush.

The day

How the class runs

  1. Register

    Send your details and we’ll confirm the next cohort dates and how to pay the registration fee.

  2. Demonstration

    Each module is demonstrated on a live model, with the reasoning behind every choice explained.

  3. Practice

    You work through the technique yourself with hands-on correction throughout.

  4. Connect

    Small groups by design — you leave with the technique and a circle of peers in the industry.

Your tutor

Janet Ayisi

A Ghanaian professional makeup artist, cosmetologist and beauty entrepreneur whose expertise spans bridal, special occasions, photoshoots, artiste and media makeup — as well as makeup education.

Janet teaches the way she works: attention to detail, an emphasis on hygiene and professionalism, and a belief that makeup should enhance who you are rather than change it.

Questions

Class details

Do I need my own kit?
Not to attend. Products are provided for the practical sessions, and we’ll talk through what to buy first when you’re building your own kit — and what can wait.
Do I need a model?
Ask when you register. Depending on the cohort, students either pair up to practise on each other or work with a provided model. We’ll confirm before the day.
Is there a certificate?
Get in touch when registering and we’ll confirm what the current cohort includes.
How do I pay the registration fee?
Register your interest through the form or WhatsApp, and we’ll send payment details along with the confirmed dates for the next cohort.
Can you run a private or group class?
Yes. Private one-to-one sessions and closed group bookings can be arranged around your schedule — message us with what you have in mind.
